Zepto PO Desk

Upload Zepto PO exports and the Zepto SKU-wise sales and inventory sheet. Each New PO is checked in date order against live stock, POs already in transit and the sales rate. The Payouts tab estimates what moves in a month: Zepto to reseller, and reseller to D'Chica.

Does each PO overstock?

New POs are checked oldest first. Each one sees live stock, every Open and In Transit PO, and the accepted part of earlier New POs. A line is cut where it takes the SKU past the overstock limit. Set a PO's status in the table: once it's In Transit, later POs are checked against it.
